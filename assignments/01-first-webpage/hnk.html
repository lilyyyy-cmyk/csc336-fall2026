Houseki no Kuni (宝石の国), or Land of the Lustrous in English, is an animanga (anime & manga) created by the mangaka Haruko Ichikawa. It follows the story of a young gem, Phosphophyllite surrounded by their fellow gems spending their days fighting against lunarians; in short.
