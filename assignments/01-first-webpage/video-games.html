I enjoy many video games which I download and play mostly through the Steam Game Engine. The genres I play range from rpgs, visual novels, rougelike, fps/tps, pvp and more.

Yume Nikki (ゆめにっき)

Yume Nikki is an wanderer rpg in the sense that most of the game entails walking around and exploring new areas. While exploring, you will come across a range of items that you can equip to Madotsuki (the MC) to guide you. For more information refer to this link: Yume Nikki Fandom Wiki

Your Turn to Die (キミガシネ)

Your Turn to Die is an interactive visual novel in which you play as the MC, Sara Chidouin, who has been forced to play in a death by majority game. Your choices matter in what routes you follow during your play-through. For more information, refer to this link: Yttd Fandom Wiki

Balatro

Balatro is a rougelike card game in which you build your deck of cards, amplify your score with jokers and other assets to get through antes (three blinds). You can score by playing scoring hands (like a flush). The goal is to get as far as you can on each run. For more information, refer to this link: Balatro Fandom Wiki
